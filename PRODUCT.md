# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two people, two roles.

- **The child (plays), 3–6 years old.** Often cannot read. Taps with imprecise fingers, sometimes with a palm or several fingers at once, sometimes holds a finger down. Plays on a parent's phone or tablet in landscape, at home, in a short session of 5–15 minutes. Includes children with speech delay (ЗРР), who are the core audience of the method: they need a slower pace, more repetition, patient hints. Includes colour-blind children.
- **The parent (chooses and supervises).** Picks the game in the Yandex Games catalogue, wants visible benefit for speech, fears shock ads, accidental purchases, overstimulation and "zombie" screen time. Reaches settings only through the parent gate.

## Product Purpose

A talking picture book: the voice asks the child to find a character on a big illustrated scene, names it, says what it does, and invites the child to repeat the word aloud ("Скажи: бе-лоч-ка!"). Tasks follow the natural order of speech development: object → sound → action → attribute → place → feeling. Success: the child plays a whole scene without adult help, repeats words aloud, comes back the next day; the parent sees benefit and trusts the game.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, bookish. Like a good paper wimmelbook read aloud by a kind kindergarten teacher: gouache and paper-collage art, muted warm palette, unhurried voice. Every touch is answered, nothing punishes. Joy comes from discovery ("чпок!", the character comes alive), not from flashing effects.

## Anti-references

- The "red ocean" of kids' games on Yandex Games: all-in-one mini-game bundles, colouring + puzzles + sorting, constant flashing, loud neon palettes, licensed characters.
- Games for 7–12 (Obby, Sprunki, "brainrot" memes): chaos, speed, screaming sounds.
- "Занятия с логопедом" apps: worksheets, scores, red crosses, a lesson that feels like a test.
- Anything with timers, Game Over, lives, penalties, streak pressure, loot-box style rewards or purchase buttons a child can reach.

## Design Principles

1. **Voice, not text.** Every instruction a child needs is spoken; on-screen text is for parents only.
2. **Zero negative.** Wrong taps are small discoveries (the thing names itself), there is no failure state, the end is always three stars.
3. **Forgiving hands.** Targets are larger than they look, only the primary touch counts, accidental and rage taps turn into something pleasant, exits that end the game sit out of reach or behind a hold.
4. **Calm over thrill.** Motion and sound reward, they never overstimulate; one thing moves at a time where possible.
5. **The parent stays in charge.** Settings, purchases, ads and the way out of the game are behind the parent gate or clearly adult-facing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Non-readers: nothing essential depends on reading; icons and voice carry meaning.
- Imprecise motor control: hit areas ≥ 52 CSS px and 25–30 % larger than the art; primary pointer only; tolerance for long presses and palms.
- Speech delay (ЗРР): unhurried voice, echo pause after "Скажи", repeated cues, hints that escalate gently (12 s → 25 s → firefly).
- Colour blindness: colour tasks ("найди всё красное") must also work through shape and voice; hints and found-state never rely on hue alone.
- Reduced motion respected for non-essential animation; volume of music/sfx/voice adjustable separately; landscape only with a rotate prompt.
- Target: WCAG 2.2 AA for parent-facing text and controls.
